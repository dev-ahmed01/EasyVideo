# Furnihub B2B — stock footage benchmark

Goal: qualified office procurement enquiries that include the requested chair count. Test whether genuine independent B-roll helps, while keeping exactly the same real product footage and audio.

## Short script (approx. 20 seconds, conversational)

"Setting up an office? The chair count adds up fast. Furnihub can show you seating options. Here's the actual chair, up close. Tell us how many seats you're planning, and we'll help you check what's available. DM OFFICE with your chair count."

Verify inventory and service area before publishing. Avoid made-up warranties, discounts and installation testimonials.

## Controlled A/B test

A (control): prior real product and office footage only, with final selected narration and CTA.

B (treatment): identical narrative, voiceover, duration, real product clips and CTA. Change only two office-atmosphere scenes to 2 independently downloaded licensed stock files.

Upload stock as Footage > Illustrative licensed stock > Office atmosphere. The application rejects stock as real inventory, product hero, functionality or customer proof.

Compare average watch time, shares/reach and genuine chair-count enquiries per 1,000 plays after publishing.

## Limitation

The provider pages and commercial-use licensing were verified, but actual stock-video binary downloads were blocked in the current environment. The new benchmark implementation is ready, but a true stock-vs-no-stock export cannot be asserted until licensed video files are available in EasyVideo.
